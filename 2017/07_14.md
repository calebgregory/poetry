# 2017-07-14, 2024-09-18

you was a bone explosion written in four parts on
railways sideways and open to the new nude groove of
Danny Glover beaming in Beloved, where wet heat smothered,
cicadas ribbing in the background.
look right on into the camera now,
zoom slowly into a shouting mouth
soundless --
don't startle the babies curled against the spine.

while under, in sand and clay, worms worm you into dirt.
we rub our eyes unhurt
and we hope for better weather --
i think i should have said it sooner, thought i'd write it in a letter,
but it never felt like the right time,
and the thought-crime of perfection blocked my writer,
and i never wrote you.
i painted you a watercolor but i didn't like it.
i sketched it in graphite and went to bed with plans to finish.
you were gone two nights later.

--

in my chest,
the stairwell swells with pages pushing to the shelves in red letters
and while i sleep, they write your name on yellowed paper, pen filled with blood
from before the flood that will wipe our muddy bodies wide open --
from before the flood that will make us clean.

there in blood on yellow paper:
a record of labor, of you working double shifts from 9 to 9
the log of a journey: the path a fibrous braid of jerky turning:
the labor flame
burning the layman brain into a knot:
see their grand invasive, invisible hands weaving
a vast invisible net to catch the lesser minds of men, and women, too,
bending thought toward their willing:
skill-building and productive.

taking your name as an implement, they welded it into
a golden map of hell.

a rolodex of pages,
wages we both thought were worth it;
years later, by total accident
we discovered secret words were written
on those cards right at the corners
seen by flip book animation:

a love note that reads, "don't go here.
this ground, it is well covered. do something else;
believe me, i lived through it already."

--

stuck in a book as bookmark:
a withered postcard picture of our great grandmother
framed by a doorway,
thumbing beads to memorize the maker's names,
her eyes focused on the distance
dusty and dry
her skin smooth
and unwrinkled.

a kitten standing in the doorway
looks mutely at the camera.

--

and when the carbon air is where we have no more twigs or leaves
we will rip the pages free to catch our last sparks:
even then, we'll have to eat.
even one last meal
is a meal, is a meal
praise God for this meal
praise God
for this meal
praise God

--

now turn and adjust.

the luster is well worn by her.
the kitten purs and curls his neck into her palm
forgets the grass against his nose
in praise of her pulse
that thumps and lulls him soft to sleep.
he keeps
his mouth smiling sweet
quietly
for a minute or two.

the breeze whispers in the pines.

--

it may be there will be no water.
when we are covered in dirt, wind will whip us clean
on our finally family vacation.

--

so taper your pantlegs smooth, I told you once, you're clean
unlearn neurotic words like dirty - do you remember when that meant nothing?
perfection: a way of being _here_, absorbed in _us all_.
perfection: not a standard against which you are measured -- no, not a rule.
that was a midgame rule-change for the losing team's advantage --
they took the word and changed its meaning to con you into believing you are wrong,
but not so:
  just deceived by
    those who were deceived by
      those who were deceived by
        those.
and closing in on a picture of a depiction of an affinity for making fiction
we watch the maker stitch the situation grave and full of mind
tear the rind free from the fruit and squeeze the juice over the flame...

what's he stitching?

what are the ways of being?
  we will learn them all.
full of mind, severed and blind
  sometimes
like fingernails full of dirt when --
here comes a fingernail clipper...

we shed our skin: new growth.
God trims our fingernails,
and we cry
and we learn how to fall
and we slowly get quicker.

through thick and thin, begin again --

and slipping backwards against the pants pressed flat, we laugh
because forever shakes hands between our eyes.

and this is our justice, our counteraction to this mess:
to stand upon the earth
together.
standing forever
  for a time.

i'm sorry i rambled;
i'm sorry i missed you;
but i know you now.
what you are, what you were, i give back to you.
God bless you, God bless you,
God bless you.
